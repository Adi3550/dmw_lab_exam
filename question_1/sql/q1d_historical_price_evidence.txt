Q1(d) - HISTORICAL PRICE EVIDENCE
==================================

Historical prices are obtained from the PostgreSQL
price_revisions table.

Product matching uses:

product_code
+
business_date
+
products.valid_from
+
products.valid_to

Then:

product_sk
+
business_date
+
price_revisions.effective_from
+
price_revisions.effective_to

This ensures that the price applicable to the reporting period
is selected rather than automatically using the current price.
