DASHBOARD DIMENSIONS
--------------------

The dashboard query supports slicing revenue by:

- Store
- Product category
- Day of week
- Month


PRODUCT HISTORY
---------------

product_code is not used as the final product identifier.

The historical product is resolved using:

product_code
+
business_date
+
valid_from / valid_to

The resulting product_sk is stored in fact_sales.


REVENUE DEFINITION
------------------

Revenue includes:

SALE
RETURN
DISCOUNT
VOID

Revenue excludes:

TAX
TENDER


DESIGN BENEFIT
--------------

Store, category and product descriptive information is stored
in dimension tables instead of being repeated on every sales
line in the large fact table.
